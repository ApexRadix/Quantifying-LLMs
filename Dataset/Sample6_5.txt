used to use type writers, which significantly increased their memory
power. Nowadays all that has been replacd; from text to message
writing is all done single-handedly by automation

how to frame a paragraph and write by themselves because they
rely entirely on AI tools

humans, from reimagining and creating mind boggling ideas

stages of cancer, AI is helping every where. But if people don't
go back to the traditional ways of doing things the day is
not far when the next generation will be utterly weak in
vocabulary and speech. The parts of human brain responsible
for speech, talk and memories are weakening in youngsters
AI is an emerging technology used in 99% of feilds
today. More advanced technology may emerge but
