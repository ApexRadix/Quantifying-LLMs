The evolution of originality: Defining human creativity in the age of
generative AI
Creativity is something humans have always taken pride in. The
idea that we can imagine something and bring it to life - through
writing, music or art has always set us apart. But lately this idea
is being questioned. With machines now being able to generate
content that looks creative, we are forced to rethink what originality
actually means
For a long time, originality was understood as a producing something
new and personal. But even before any technology came in to the picture
human creativity was never truly from scratch. writers borrowed styles
Painters were inspired by other painters, musician built traditions
What made their work original was not that it appeared out of air
but that it carried a personal perspective. It reflected how a particular
person saw the world at a particular time
That is still core of what originality means. When a person creates
something there is an emotional and intellectual process behind it. There are
choices being made what to included, what to leave out, what tone to
use, what message to send, these decisions come from lived experience
personality and thought that cannot simply to automated.
so perhaps this era is not the end of originality it is remainder
of what made it valuable in the first place. Originality was never judge
just about being different. It was always about being real.
