The Evolution of Originality : Defining Human
Creativity in the Age of Generative AI .
Creativity is something humans have always taken pride in.
The idea that we can imagine something and bring it to
life - through writing, music, or art - has always set us
apart. But lately, this idea is being questioned. With
machines now able to generate content that looks creative,
we are forced to rethink what originality actually
means.
For a longtime, originality was understood as
producing something new and personal. But even before any
technology came into the picture, human creativity was
never truly "from scratch." Writers borrowed styles,
painters were inspired by other painters, musicians built
on traditions. what made their work original was not
that it appeared out of thin air, but that it carried
a personal perspective. It reflected ~~ap~~ how a particular
person saw the world at a particular time.
That is still core of what originality means. When a
person creates something there is an emotional and
intellectual process behind it. There are choices being made
what include, what to leave out, what tone to use,
what message to send - these decisions come from lived
experiances, personality and thought. that is something
that cannot simply be automated.
So perhaps this era is not the of originality -
it is reminder of what made it valuable in the
first place. Originality was never just about being
different. It was always about being real.
